# MindYourTrip AI

> **Plan Smarter. Travel Together.**

MindYourTrip AI is an AI-powered collaborative travel planning platform that helps travelers organize trips, manage budgets, split expenses fairly, and collaborate with friends in one place.

Unlike traditional AI trip planners, ** MindYourTrip AI acts as an intelligent assistant—not the decision maker.** It provides personalized recommendations, budget insights, and predictive analytics based on user preferences and previous trips, while keeping every final decision in the hands of the travelers.

## ✨ Features

- 🤖 AI-powered travel recommendations
- 👥 Collaborative trip workspace & group chat
- 💰 Smart expense tracking and fair bill splitting
- 📊 Budget analysis and spending insights
- 📅 Shared itinerary planning
- 📸 AI receipt scanner *(Planned)*
- 🧠 Personalized suggestions from past trips
- 🗳️ Polls and voting for group decisions

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Frontend:** React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Vite
- **Backend:** Node.js, Express *(Planned)*
- **Database:** Supabase/PostgreSQL *(Planned)*
- **AI:** LLMs, NLP, Predictive Analytics, Generative AI

## 🚀 Vision

To simplify group travel through collaboration and AI-powered insights while ensuring that **travelers always stay in control of their journey.**
